# پت آباد — Business/FAQ knowledge, card parity, clarification cleanup

Scope: PetAbad only (`/petabad`, `/m/petabad`, `/petabad/floating`, `petabad-agent`). Flowcart/GPTCommerce and Shift untouched.

## 1. Petabad FAQ as a source of truth

The uploaded file holds 30 question/answer pairs. They will be stored as a versioned knowledge base and become the only allowed source for business answers.

**Storage**
- New table `pet_faqs`: `id`, `question`, `answer`, `category`, `subtopic`, `tags[]`, `keywords[]`, `phone_numbers[]`, `is_active`, `version`, `updated_at`, `embedding vector`, plus a Persian-normalized search column (same FTS + `pg_trgm` + HNSW cosine setup used for `pet_products`). GRANTs: `select` to `anon`/`authenticated`, `all` to `service_role`; RLS on with a read-only public policy.
- Seed all 30 entries verbatim from the file (answers are never rewritten in the database, including the phone numbers, IBAN/card details, and the badly-encoded rows, which get cleaned to readable Persian without changing meaning).
- New RPC `pet_faq_search(query, top_k)`: normalized full-text + trigram + vector, returning entries with a similarity score. Embeddings generated by a small `petabad-faq-embeddings` function, re-runnable when FAQ text changes.

**Taxonomy (derived from the actual file, not the example list)**
1. `ordering_account` — signup, phone/WhatsApp/Instagram/app ordering, per-order limits (e.g. max two cat litters).
2. `payment` — card-to-card details, Saman gateway, SnappPay instalments, credit activation, credit limit shortfall.
3. `discounts` — Petabad coupon field vs SnappPay coupon panel.
4. `shipping_cost` — Tehran express by weight, Tehran courier tiers, non-Tehran post/DigiExpress calculated at checkout.
5. `shipping_timing` — normal vs fast windows, 7-day selection, Friday/holiday exclusion, how to view scheduling.
6. `order_tracking` — order status in account, support line, postal tracking code.
7. `order_changes` — no add/remove items, cancel only at «ثبت سفارش», address/date change rules at «بسته‌بندی».
8. `returns` — free-return conditions (unopened seal, 7 working days), damaged goods, non-authentic goods, how to file a return.
9. `refunds` — 7–10 working days, SnappPay 5–10 working days, IBAN settlement 72 working hours.
10. `guarantees` — authenticity, expiry-date guarantee, site-match guarantee, live stock/price accuracy.
11. `store_info_trust` — e-trust badge and union membership, no in-person purchase.
12. `support_channels` — phone numbers, online chat icon, WhatsApp/Instagram, after-sales desk.

Each seeded row carries its category plus colloquial keyword variants (مرجوعی، پس دادن، چند روزه میرسه، پرداخت در محل، کرایه ارسال …) so short and misspelled Persian questions still retrieve.

## 2. Agent behaviour

- New tool `petabad_business_lookup(question, topics?)` in `petabad-agent`, backed by `pet_faq_search`. It returns the matched entries with their exact answers and their coverage judgement.
- Intent routing before retrieval: product / pet-care advice / business / mixed. A business intent never reaches product search; a mixed question runs both tools and the answer keeps the two sources visibly separate.
- Coverage discipline in the answer prompt: **fully answered** → answer from the retrieved text; **partially answered** → answer only the supported part and say plainly which detail Petabad has not specified; **not answered** → say the information isn't established and point to the real support channels from the FAQ. No invented times, fees, conditions, or promises; no general e-commerce knowledge; conditional wording («امکان دارد») never becomes a promise.
- The business path never calls the web-lookup tool, and product data is never used to infer policy.
- Conversation context: the last business topic is kept in the PetAbad shopping context, so «یعنی تا چند روز وقت دارم؟», «چند روزه میرسه؟», «شرایطش چیه؟» resolve against the previous business topic or the products just shown instead of starting a new search. Clarification is only asked when the context genuinely has no subject.
- Catalog-scope questions («کلاً چه برندهایی دارید؟», «چه محصولاتی برای سگ دارید؟») answer from catalog facets, while «از بین اینا …» answers from the shown result set — the two are not conflated.
- Answers stay short, neutral, and non-promotional; every business answer logs which FAQ ids it used, so it is traceable.

**Testing** — for each taxonomy branch: exact question, paraphrase, colloquial short form, misspelling, follow-up («چند روز؟»), pronoun reference, mixed product+business, and an adversarial uncovered question (installments beyond SnappPay, in-store pickup, working hours) where the correct result is refusing to invent.

## 3. Issue 2 — cards don't match the recommended products

The reply listed eight products in four groups but only two cards rendered. Cause to confirm from the request log before fixing, then enforce parity in `petabad-agent`: the visible numbered list and the returned card set must be the same rows in the same order. When the model's `SELECTED_IDS` line is missing, partial, or contains ids outside the candidate set, the card set falls back to the grouped candidate rows the model was told to write about (up to the 9-item cap) instead of shipping a shorter card list, and numbering restarts at 1 across the whole grouped answer. Verified live with the exact German-Shepherd bundle request on the floating surface.

## 4. Issue 3 — duplicate summary card after answering a question card

In `src/components/petabad/ClarificationBlocks.tsx` an answered card collapses into a grey summary that repeats the shopper's own reply, which then also shows as the user bubble. The answered card will render nothing at all; only the shopper's message remains. Applies to desktop, mobile, and floating threads.
